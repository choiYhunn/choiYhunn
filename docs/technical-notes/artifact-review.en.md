The search covered filenames and selected code content in Documents, Downloads, project backups and Codex/Claude work folders, plus the Windows home file. It inspected **60 ZIP indexes and 84 small Python/Arduino source members** from the education/Downloads corpus. It was not a complete audit of every large backup/archive format or of the workshop HPC disk.

The earlier distribution-tree comparison omitted the HPC application outside that tree and the historical session's generated code, edits and attached logs. Recovery hashes, exact local paths and raw evidence remain outside public repositories. The public notes contain new prose, original diagrams and source links. Courseware, third-party figures, complete assignment prompts/answers, commercial libraries, certificates, student identifiers, school email, device addresses and company material are excluded.
